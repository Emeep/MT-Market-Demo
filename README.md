
  # MT Market -
A secondhand marketplace platform (web + mobile app) built to fight online fraud in Thailand. It uses 10-dimension AI verification to screen scammers before a deal starts, and market analysis to show whether an item is worth buying.

Built for **UniHack 2026** (theme: *AI & Safety*).
## The Problem
Fraud in buying and selling goods and services is the most reported online crime in Thailand: 482,724 cases, 56.74% of the top-5 online fraud categories. PromptPay transfers are instant and almost impossible to recover, so protection has to happen *before* the money moves.

## How It Works
1. **10-dimension AI verification** scores buyers and sellers: account age, ID verification, cross-platform history, brand badge, image forensics, bot detection, volume-to-review ratio, evasion detection, fraud database check, and deal score.
2. **MT Market escrow** holds the buyer's payment until they confirm the item arrived as described.
3. **Protection Premium** (optional) refunds the buyer in full if the item is defective or not as described.
4. **Market analysis** shows whether an item is a good buy right now.

## Key Features
- Bidirectional trust score (protects buyers and sellers)
- Fraud database fed by one-tap community reports
- Evasion detection (flags "add me on LINE", "transfer directly", and similar)
- Transparent "why this score?" explanations
- Escrow-based payment flow

## Target Users
Everyday buyers and sellers of secondhand goods in Thailand, especially first-time sellers, students, and people less familiar with digital payments.
